**[City, State] — [Date]** — DoomNotes, a new productivity app, today announced its official launch on iOS and Android. **The Problem:**

Social media platforms like Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube have become major sources of educational content. However, users struggle to retain what they watch:

- 73% of users bookmark videos to \"watch later\" but never return
- Average re-watch rate for bookmarked videos: 12%
- Time wasted searching for previously watched content: 2+ hours/week

**The Solution:**

DoomNotes solves this by:

1. **One-tap capture:** Users share videos directly from Instagram/TikTok/YouTube to DoomNotes
2. **Auto-transcription:** Whisper AI converts speech to text in seconds
3. **Smart organization:** Notes are automatically sorted by topic (investing, color grading, video editing, etc.)
4. **Instant search:** Users can search across all their notes by keyword or topic

**Key Features:**

- **Offline-first:** All notes stored locally, works without internet
- **Privacy-focused:** No account required, no data selling
- **Free:** Core features are 100% free (optional premium tier for cloud sync)
- **Cross-platform:** iOS, Android, with desktop app (Windows/Mac) in development
