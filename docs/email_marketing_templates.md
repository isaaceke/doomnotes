## Best Practices

### Subject Lines
- Keep under 50 characters
- Use emojis sparingly (1 max)
- Create curiosity or urgency
- Personalize when possible

### Timing
- Launch emails: Tuesday-Thursday, 10 AM - 2 PM
- Newsletters: First Monday of month, 9 AM
- Engagement nudges: Wednesday, 3 PM

### Frequency
- Don't email more than 1x/week
- Monthly newsletter is safe
- Let users unsubscribe easily

### Metrics to Track
- Open rate (target: >30%)
- Click-through rate (target: >5%)
- Unsubscribe rate (target: <1%)
- Conversion rate (for premium emails)
