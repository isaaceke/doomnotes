- [ ] Play Store listing completed (name, description, category)
- [ ] Screenshots uploaded (iOS: 5, Android: 2-8)
- [ ] App icon uploaded (iOS: 1024x1024, Android: 512x512)
- [ ] Privacy policy URL hosted (use docs/PRIVACY_POLICY.md)
- [ ] Terms of service URL hosted (use docs/TERMS_OF_SERVICE.md)

- [ ] App Privacy labels completed (App Store Connect)

- [ ] App tested on iOS (latest version)

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

### Don't:
- ❌ Launch without testing on real devices
- ❌ Ignore negative reviews (respond to all)
- ❌ Over-promise features you can't deliver
- ❌ Spam social media (provide value, don't just promote)
- ❌ Give up if launch is slow (most apps grow slowly at first)

### Do:
- ✅ Respond to every user (builds loyalty)
- ✅ Iterate based on feedback (show you listen)
- ✅ Celebrate small wins (100 downloads, first 5-star review, etc.)
- ✅ Stay consistent (post daily, even if engagement is low)
- ✅ Focus on retention (better to keep users than constantly acquire new ones)
