## Introduction

DoomNotes ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. DoomNotes does not
require account creation. All data is stored locally on your device by
default.

## Information We Collect

**Links and text you save.** When you share a link (from TikTok, YouTube,
Instagram, or elsewhere) or paste one manually, we store the link, any
caption/text that came with it, and the topic it was filed under, on your
device.

**We do not:**
- transcribe, download, or process the video/post content itself
- collect your name, email, phone number, or location
- collect usage analytics or crash reports
- show ads

## Local Storage

Your notes are stored on-device using the Isar database. This app does not
currently encrypt that local database. Nothing leaves your device unless
you use Export (which creates a JSON file you explicitly choose to share)
or unless you turn on optional cloud sync (see below).

## Optional Cloud Sync

[If you have enabled cloud sync per docs/ENABLE_CLOUD_SYNC.md, describe it

If you create an account, your notes are synced to a Firebase project
we operate, scoped to your account (Firestore security rules restrict each
user to their own data). This is standard cloud storage, protected by
authentication and access rules — it is not end-to-end encrypted. You can
sign out and continue using the app locally at any time; deleting your
account deletes your synced data.

## Third-Party Content

When you tap a saved link, we open it in your browser or the relevant
app (TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, etc.) via the OS. We don't access your
accounts on those platforms. Content you open there is subject to that
platform's own privacy policy.

## Your Choices

- **Delete a note:** open it → menu → Delete.
- **Export your data:** Settings → Export / Import → Export all notes.
- **Delete everything:** uninstalling the app removes all local data (and,
  if cloud sync is enabled, deleting your account removes your cloud data).

## Children's Privacy

DoomNotes is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly
collect information from them.
